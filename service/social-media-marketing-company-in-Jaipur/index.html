What Is Social Media Management?

Social media management is the ongoing work of planning, creating, publishing and analysing content across your social platforms, and engaging with the people who interact with it. It covers strategy, content calendars, reels and design, captions, community replies and monthly performance reviews.

It differs from paid social ads. Management builds your organic presence and reputation over time, while ads pay to reach specific audiences quickly. The strongest brands use both together.

THE NEW RULES OF SOCIAL

What's Changed on Social Media, and Why Does It Matter?

UPDATE 01
Your Posts Can Now Show Up on Google

Since July 2025, public posts and reels from Instagram business and creator accounts can appear in Google and Bing search results. Your captions, alt text and on-screen text now affect discoverability far beyond Instagram, which makes social content part of your search strategy.

UPDATE 02
Original Content Wins, Reposts Lose Reach

Instagram now limits recommendations for accounts that mostly share content they didn't create or haven't meaningfully changed. This now applies to photos and carousels as well as reels, so reposting trends or stock content no longer grows an account.

UPDATE 03
The Audience Is Huge, and Highly Specific

India has over 50 crore Instagram users, and the largest group is aged 25 to 34. Reaching them isn't the challenge. Standing out in a crowded feed with content that speaks directly to your buyer is.

Should Your Content Be in English, Hindi or Hinglish?

It depends on how your audience talks, not how you'd like to sound. A doctor explaining symptoms to local patients often connects better in Hinglish, while a B2B brand on LinkedIn usually needs polished English.

We write scripts and captions in English, Hindi or Romanised Hinglish based on your audience, and keep your brand voice consistent across all of them.

FAQ

Social Media Management: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do social media management services cost?+
Costs depend on the number of platforms, posts and reels per month, whether shoots are needed and how much community management is involved. A single-platform plan for a local business costs less than a multi-platform plan with regular video production. We share a clear package after understanding your goals.
How many posts should my brand publish each month?+
Consistency matters more than volume. Most brands do well with a steady weekly mix of reels, carousels and stories. We recommend a posting frequency based on your goals, audience and the quality of content you can sustain.
Do I need to appear on camera or shoot the content myself?+
Not always. Some brands work best with founder or expert-led videos, while others rely on product shoots, designs and creator content. We script, direct and edit, and can plan shoots so your time commitment stays manageable.
How long does it take to see growth on social media?+
Engagement and content quality usually improve within the first one to two months. Steady follower growth, stronger brand recall and consistent enquiries typically build over three to six months of consistent posting, faster if supported by ads.
Can my Instagram posts help my business show up on Google?+
Yes. Since July 2025, public content from Instagram business and creator accounts can appear in Google search results. Writing keyword-aware captions, alt text and clear on-screen text helps your posts get found by people searching outside Instagram.
Do you manage social media for doctors and clinics?+
Yes. We create educational content for doctors and healthcare brands in English, Hindi or Hinglish, following NMC ethical advertising guidelines. That means no misleading claims, no guaranteed outcomes and responsible handling of patient information.
What's the difference between social media management and social media ads?+
Management builds your organic presence through regular content and community engagement. Ads pay to put specific content in front of targeted audiences. Organic content builds trust and recall, while ads speed up reach and sales. They work best together.
Will I get to approve content before it's posted?+
Yes. We share the monthly calendar, scripts, designs and captions in advance. Nothing goes live without your approval, and we build your feedback into future content.
