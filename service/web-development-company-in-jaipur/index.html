THE NEW STANDARD

What Does a Business Website Need to Get Right Today?

A good-looking website is no longer enough. Google, AI search tools and your customers now judge your site on much more than design.

Speed and Responsiveness

Google's Core Web Vitals measure how fast your pages load and how quickly they react to a tap or click. Since March 2024, responsiveness is measured by Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and a good score means reacting in under 200 milliseconds. Heavy themes and bloated plugins often fail this.

Mobile-First Design

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone, and Google evaluates your site primarily through its mobile version. Every layout, button and form must work comfortably on a small screen first.

Readable by AI Search

Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and other AI tools need clean, well-structured content and schema markup to understand what you offer. Websites built mostly with heavy scripts and images can be hard for these systems to read.

Privacy-Ready Forms and Tracking

India's DPDP Rules, notified in November 2025, bring notice and consent requirements into force by May 2027. Lead forms, cookies and tracking should be built with clear consent in mind from the start.

Web Development in Jaipur: Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a web development company in Jaipur charge for a website?+
It depends on the number of pages, design complexity, platform and features such as ecommerce, booking or integrations. A simple business website costs far less than a custom web app or a large online store. We share a clear, itemised quote after understanding your requirements.
How long does it take to build a website?+
Standard business websites and Shopify storefronts typically take two to four weeks. Larger ecommerce stores, custom-built sites and web applications take longer, depending on features and how quickly content and feedback are shared.
Should I choose Shopify, WordPress or a custom website?+
Shopify is usually best for online stores, WordPress for content-rich business websites and custom development for unique features or web applications. We recommend a platform based on your goals, budget and how you plan to manage the site.
Will my new website rank on Google?+
Every site we build has a strong technical SEO foundation, including speed, structure, schema and clean URLs. Ranking for competitive keywords also needs ongoing content and authority building, which our SEO team can handle after launch.
Can you redesign my existing website without losing rankings?+
Yes. We map your existing URLs, set up proper redirects, keep valuable content and check your search performance after launch. Careful planning is what prevents traffic drops during a redesign.
Who should own my domain and hosting?+
You should. We recommend registering your domain and hosting accounts in your own business name, so you always keep full control of your website, regardless of which agency you work with.
Do you provide website maintenance after launch?+
Yes. We offer post-launch support, including updates, security checks, speed optimisation and content changes, so your website keeps performing well over time.
Will my website be ready for AI search tools like Google AI Overviews?+
We build with clean code, clear content structure and schema markup, which make your site easier for AI tools to understand. Appearing in AI answers also depends on content quality and authority, which we can strengthen through ongoing SEO.
