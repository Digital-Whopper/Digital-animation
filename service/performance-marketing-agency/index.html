What Does a Performance Marketing Agency Actually Do?

A performance marketing agency runs paid campaigns where success is measured by actions, such as sales, leads, app installs or bookings, rather than impressions or reach. It plans the strategy, builds the ads, sets up conversion tracking and keeps optimising budgets toward the campaigns that bring the best return.

In simple terms: you don't pay for "visibility". You invest in outcomes, and every decision is guided by data on what those outcomes cost.

THE RULES HAVE CHANGED

Why Are Ads Getting More Expensive but Not More Effective?

If your ad costs keep rising while results stay flat, you're not alone. Three big shifts have changed how paid advertising works.

SHIFT 01
More Brands Competing for Feeds

Digital already accounts for roughly 59% of India's total ad spend, and is projected to approach 70% by 2027. More advertisers in the same auctions means higher costs for anyone running unoptimised campaigns.

SHIFT 02
Creative Is Now the Targeting

Meta's AI-driven ad delivery system, Andromeda, has shifted the focus from narrow audience targeting to creative diversity. The platform now decides who sees your ad largely based on the ad itself. Brands running the same few creatives are being left behind.

SHIFT 03
Privacy Rules Are Tightening

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified in November 2025, with notice and consent provisions phasing in by May 2027. Combined with browser and device privacy changes, this makes first-party data and accurate, consent-aware tracking essential.

Our approach is built for this new reality: broad, AI-friendly campaign structures, a steady pipeline of fresh creative and tracking you can actually trust.

CREATIVE ENGINE

Why Is Creative Testing the Biggest Lever in Paid Ads Today?

With platforms using AI to find buyers, the ad itself does most of the targeting. That makes creative quality and variety the single biggest driver of results.

  • ✓ Concept-led testing: We test genuinely different angles, such as problem, outcome, social proof, founder story and comparison, not just new colours or headlines.
  • ✓ Mixed formats: Static, carousel, short video, UGC-style and creator content, so each ad reaches a different type of buyer.
  • ✓ Audience psychology: Hooks and messaging built around what your customer fears, wants and believes, using their own language.
  • ✓ Regular refresh cycles: New creative goes live before old ads fatigue, keeping costs stable as you scale.
MEASUREMENT YOU CAN TRUST

How Do You Know Which Ads Are Really Making Money?

Platform dashboards often over-report results, while missing tracking makes good campaigns look bad. We fix measurement first, because every optimisation decision depends on it.

  • ✓ Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI): Server-side tracking that recovers conversions browser-based tracking misses
  • ✓ Google Ads and GA4 setup: Correct conversion actions, enhanced conversions and clean UTM structure
  • ✓ Blended metrics: We look at overall marketing efficiency and customer acquisition cost, not just each platform's own ROAS claim
  • ✓ Funnel leak checks: Landing page speed, checkout drop-offs and form friction that quietly waste ad spend
  • ✓ Consent-aware data practices: Tracking set up with India's evolving data protection rules in mind

FAQ

Performance Marketing: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between performance marketing and digital marketing?+
Digital marketing is the broad umbrella that includes SEO, social media, content, email and paid ads. Performance marketing is the part focused on paid campaigns that are measured and optimised by specific outcomes, such as sales, leads or bookings, and the cost of getting each one.
How much does it cost to hire a performance marketing agency?+
Costs usually include a management fee for strategy, creative and optimisation, plus your ad budget spent on the platforms. The right amount depends on your goals, number of platforms and creative needs. We share a transparent quote after auditing your account and goals.
How soon will I see results from paid ads?+
Campaigns start collecting data as soon as they go live, but the first few weeks are a learning phase where platforms and testing find what works. Most brands see a clearer picture of stable performance after one to three months of consistent testing and optimisation.
Should I run Meta ads or Google ads?+
It depends on how your customers buy. Google captures people already searching for what you sell, so it suits high-intent demand. Meta creates demand by showing your product to likely buyers who weren't searching yet. Many brands get the best results using both together.
What is a good ROAS?+
There's no single answer. A good ROAS depends on your profit margins, average order value and repeat purchase rate. We calculate your break-even ROAS first, then set targets that keep campaigns profitable rather than chasing a generic number.
Why is my ROAS dropping even though nothing changed?+
Common causes include creative fatigue, rising competition in the auction, tracking gaps after privacy changes and landing page issues. Since platforms now rely heavily on creative to find buyers, running the same ads for too long is one of the most frequent reasons performance declines.
What is Conversions API, and do I need it?+
Conversions API (CAPI) sends conversion data from your server directly to Meta, instead of relying only on the browser pixel. It helps recover conversions lost to ad blockers and privacy settings, which gives the platform better data to optimise your campaigns. Most advertisers benefit from having it set up.
Can performance marketing work for small businesses?+
Yes, if the budget is focused. Smaller businesses usually do best with one platform, one clear offer and a tight funnel before expanding. We help you start where your budget can produce meaningful learning, then scale what works.
