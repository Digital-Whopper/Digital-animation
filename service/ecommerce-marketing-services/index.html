What Are Ecommerce Marketing Services?

Ecommerce marketing services cover everything that helps an online brand attract shoppers, convert them into buyers and bring them back again. That includes marketplace listing optimisation, product content, marketplace and social ads, website conversion improvements, email and WhatsApp marketing, and the tracking needed to measure what's actually profitable.
WHERE INDIAN ECOMMERCE IS HEADING

Why Should Brands Build Beyond Marketplaces Now?

India's online shopping market is changing fast, and the brands that adapt early will capture most of the growth.

TREND 01
D2C is Growing Faster Than Marketplaces

A February 2026 McKinsey report estimates India's D2C ecommerce could grow from around USD 10–12 billion in 2025 to USD 55–60 billion by 2030, roughly three times faster than traditional marketplaces.

TREND 02
Marketplaces Are Cutting Fees to Win Sellers

Flipkart waived commissions on products under ₹1,000 in late 2025, and Amazon India extended zero referral fees to over 12.5 crore products under ₹1,000 in March 2026. Lower fees mean more sellers, and more competition for the same search results.

TREND 03
Smaller Cities Are Driving Demand

Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities now account for more than 60% of ecommerce shipments, which changes how you price, message and deliver.

The takeaway: marketplaces are still essential for reach, but owning your customer relationship through your own store is what protects margins in the long run.

FAQ

Ecommerce Marketing Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Should I sell on marketplaces or build my own website?+
Ideally both. Marketplaces like Amazon and Flipkart give you instant access to shoppers who are ready to buy. Your own store lets you keep customer data, control your brand and earn better margins over time. Most growing brands use marketplaces for reach and their own store for loyalty and profit.
How much do ecommerce marketing services cost?+
Costs depend on the number of channels, products and services you need, such as listing optimisation, ads management, content or retention. Ad budgets are separate and paid directly to the platforms. We share a clear quote after auditing your store and marketplace accounts.
How do you improve product rankings on Amazon and Flipkart?+
Rankings depend on relevance, conversion rate and sales history. We improve relevance through keyword-optimised listings, lift conversion with better images, A+ content and reviews, and use sponsored ads to build sales velocity that supports organic ranking.
What is the difference between ACOS and TACOS?+
ACOS (advertising cost of sales) compares ad spend to sales generated by ads. TACOS (total advertising cost of sales) compares ad spend to your total sales, including organic. A falling TACOS usually means ads are helping you grow organic sales, not just buying them.
How can I reduce RTO and COD losses?+
Encourage prepaid orders with small incentives, confirm COD orders before shipping, validate addresses and phone numbers, and avoid ad audiences that produce low-intent orders. Clear product information and accurate delivery timelines also reduce refusals at the doorstep.
Can you help reduce cart abandonment on my Shopify store?+
Yes. We look at page speed, shipping cost transparency, payment options and checkout steps, then set up email and WhatsApp reminders and retargeting ads to bring back shoppers who left without buying.
How long does it take to see results from ecommerce marketing?+
Listing and conversion improvements can show results within a few weeks. Marketplace ads need some time to gather data and optimise. Sustainable growth in organic rankings, repeat purchases and margins usually builds over three to six months.
Do you help new brands launch online from scratch?+
Yes. We can help with marketplace account setup, listing creation, store launch, launch campaigns and early review strategy, so your first months online build a strong base instead of burning budget.
